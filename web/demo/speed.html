How much of this device's processor does the detector use? It runs the WebAssembly module on a built-in test melody and times every analysis step. Run it on the phone or computer you care about, and send the result.

instrumentqualitymean per step99th percentileworststep everyloadverdict

Load = the mean time of an analysis step divided by the time between steps; the browser also needs time for everything else. The audio thread is called every 128 samples (about 2.7 ms), so a single step that takes longer than that, in the 99th percentile or worst column, can cause a click. Verdict: good below 25% load with the 99th percentile under 2.5 ms, marginal below 50%, otherwise too slow.
